Mirrorfall

Two to four duelists, one cylinder, a bulb swinging over the table. Every shell is live, blank or a mirror, and nobody knows which until it fires.

or

How to play
  • On your turn, fire at any living duelist or at yourself.
  • Live shells wound whoever they hit. A blank does nothing, and a blank fired at yourself keeps your turn.
  • Mirror shells bounce: aimed at someone else, the wound returns to you. Aimed at yourself, it lands on the next duelist in turn order.
  • Relics are free actions: Scan, Eject, Charge, Ward, Spin. Last duelist standing wins the round, and two round wins take the duel.
